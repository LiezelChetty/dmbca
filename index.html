Our story

A local practice with a wider view.

DMB Chartered Accountants was founded by David Breen in 2002 and has operated from Maritana Gate in Waterford since then. Caroline Bannon joined in 2008 and was appointed Partner in 2015. Today the team serves clients across Waterford, the South East and beyond.
